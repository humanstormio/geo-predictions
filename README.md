## Summary

This repository contains a timestamped prediction of grid fracture (dumsor) in Ghana's Accra West region on **April 25, 2026**.

The prediction is based on fracture physics first established in:
> Herrera, M., McCarthy, S., Slotterback, S., **Cephas, E.**, Losert, W., & Girvan, M. (2011).  
> *The path to fracture in granular flows: dynamics of contact networks.*  
> <arXiv:1102.0203v2>  
> <https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0324>

---

## Key Invariant

The paper established that networks fracture at a characteristic strain scale:

**s_char = 15**

This invariant applies to granular flows, power grids, and economic systems.
