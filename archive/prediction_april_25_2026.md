# Fracture Prediction: Ghana Grid — April 25, 2026

**Author:** Emmanuel Cephas (co-author, Herrera-Cephas 2011)

**Prediction:** On or around April 25, 2026, the Ghana power grid will experience a fracture-level dumsor.

**Location:** The fracture will initiate at **Mallam substation**.

**Propagation:** The blackout will propagate to **Accra West** within minutes.

**Basis:** Herrera-Cephas (2011) established that granular contact networks fracture at a characteristic strain scale. Using analogical reasoning, I hypothesize the same invariant applies to power grids. This prediction is a test of that hypothesis.

**Triggers (publicly observable):**
- Frequency deviation below 49.8 Hz
- Voltage sag exceeding 3%
- Multiple active faults in Accra West corridor

**If these triggers align, fracture is imminent within 90 minutes.**

**Falsifiable:** If no dumsor occurs in Accra West on or around April 25, or if it initiates elsewhere, the hypothesis is wrong.
